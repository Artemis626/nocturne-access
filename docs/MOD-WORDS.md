# The mod's own words (for Francesco's review)

Written 22 Nov 2026 for the sanity check (`docs/SANITY-CHECK-PLAN.md`, section 5). The mod speaks the game's own text wherever the
game has it. These words are the mod's own, used where the game draws something only as an image or has no word at all. Each
needs Francesco's consent (CLAUDE.md). The status comes from the records (`docs/HANDOFF.md`, memory).

## Chosen or approved by Francesco

- "Way on." and the way-on lines (Shift+End, his design, 26 Oct 2026); "Way on, Strength." and the stat routes (2 Nov).
- "Way on, Magic, best Beidou Xingjun, 24 of 25" (the numbers, his request, 20 Nov); ", Mitama bonuses not counted" (20 Nov).
- "Slide" (22 Nov 2026, for the Yurakucho Tunnel's slides).
- "Door, includes an event" (an "Open Door" event with a door row, in the Doors filter; Francesco, 23 Nov 2026).
- "Entrance to X" and the filter "Entrances" on the world map (was "X entrance", "Doors"; Francesco, 23 Nov 2026).
- "leads to unexplored doors" / "no unexplored doors behind it" (an explored door, one room deep; Francesco chose the second wording, 23 Nov 2026).
- "You've been warped." (a script move from an event volume, the Diet Building's trick paintings, 22 Nov 2026).
- "No effect on X." (failed ailment or instakill, 20 Nov).
- "Block" (a null on a target, 16 Nov); "Insufficient macca" (shop rows, 18 Nov).
- "Dead" (party panel, confirmed 12 Oct); "Shock" and "Freeze" (statuses, 4 Nov); "Panic ended" and the other "... ended" lines (0.0.134).
- "Explored door" (before the door, 14 Oct); "Escalator up" / "Escalator down" (14 Oct).
- "Next step: ..." (the Obelisk time puzzle, 20 Oct); "Puzzle successfully completed, reached other side." (20 Oct).
- "Ride to elevator for next floor" (the Obelisk cube, 20 Oct).
- "Walking plane shifted: floor and ceiling have swapped places." (Kabukicho Prison, 15 Oct).
- "Autopilot off." / "Autopilot on." (the falling minigame, F4, 23 Oct).
- The Asakusa entrance marks ("7 metres north-west of Mifunashiro", his option 1, 19 Oct).
